Drag, attract or push the plot to reshape it; frozen parts come loose where you touch them. Melt (or M) lets it all go, and the lines run down and sort by weight. Pour some liquid onto a frozen plot and it flows around the lines like walls. A smaller Particle size (Liquids tab) gives finer lines.

The picture becomes the tank's background, and the liquid bumps into what is drawn on it. Nothing is uploaded.

Empty colours (the liquid passes through these)

LabVIEW tip: on Windows press Win+Shift+S, snip the front panel, then click this page and press Ctrl+V. If the liquid ignores something, raise Wall sensitivity; if it hits empty panel, lower it. Show walls tints what the liquid will hit.

Every dot is a particle that keeps checking its neighbours: too crowded and they push apart, too sparse and the same liquid pulls together. Heavier liquids sink under lighter ones. The method is from Clavet, Beaudoin and Poulin's paper Particle-based Viscoelastic Fluid Simulation (2005), the same one behind Grant Kot's Liquid Layers. This is a from-scratch JavaScript version.
