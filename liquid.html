Every dot is a particle that keeps checking its neighbours: too crowded and they push apart, too sparse and the same liquid pulls together. Heavier liquids sink under lighter ones. The method is from Clavet, Beaudoin and Poulin's paper Particle-based Viscoelastic Fluid Simulation (2005), the same one behind Grant Kot's Liquid Layers. This is a from-scratch JavaScript version.
