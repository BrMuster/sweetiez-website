Look

Every dot is a particle that keeps checking its neighbours: too crowded and they push apart, too sparse and the same liquid pulls together. Heavier liquids sink under lighter ones. The method is from Clavet, Beaudoin and Poulin's paper Particle-based Viscoelastic Fluid Simulation (2005), the same one behind Grant Kot's Liquid Layers. This is a from-scratch JavaScript version.

This page works the liquid out on the graphics card (WebGPU), so the tank can hold up to 300,000 particles. Each step the card sorts the particles into a grid of cells, so each one only checks the cells around it, and then every particle works out its own push from its neighbours at the same moment. Nothing is copied back to the page between frames: the card draws the liquid straight from its own memory. It needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.
